## ⏱️ Lesson Timeline

| Time | Duration | Segment | Core Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **10:50 – 11:05** | 15 mins | **Part 1: Matplotlib Anatomy** | Draw `Figure` vs `Axes`, explain OO interface |
| **11:05 – 11:25** | 20 mins | **Part 2: Distribution** | Histogram vs KDE vs Boxplot + **Analytic Discussion 1** (Bimodal trap) |

| **12:05 – 12:20** | 15 mins | **Part 5: Multivariate & Pair Plot** | `pairplot` exploration (**Analytic Discussion 5**) & Geo lollipop map |
| **12:20 – 12:30** | 10 mins | **Synthesis & Wrap-up** | Master Chart Selection Matrix on Whiteboard |

## 💡 Key Pedagogical Instructions
1. **Students have all code pre-written** in [data_visualization_intro.ipynb](file:///d:/GitHub/Data-science-class/class_02_data_visualization_intro/data_visualization_intro.ipynb).
